# Privacy Policy — Simple Time Tracker

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

**Developer:** GL Labs

**Contact:** hello@gl-labs.dev

Thank you for using Simple Time Tracker. This policy explains what
information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We
designed the app to be **local-first**: your data stays on your device and is
not sent to us.

## 1. Information we collect

Simple Time Tracker does **not** require an account and does **not** collect
any personal information from you over a network.

The app stores data **locally on your device** so you can track your work
time. This includes:

- **Work sessions** — check-in and check-out times, break durations, and the
  times you start and end breaks.
- **Session details you enter** — project name, hourly rate, notes, and tags.
- **Location (optional)** — if you enable location capture, the app records
  the latitude, longitude, and estimated accuracy for each check-in,
  check-out, or break. This is used only to tag your own work sessions and to
  let you open a saved location in your map app. You can turn this off at any
  time and delete stored locations from Settings.
- **Settings** — your hourly rate, currency, language, theme, notification
  preferences, and time-rounding preference.

All of this data is stored on your device in the app's private storage. We
have no way to access it.

## 2. What we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or contact
  details.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We do **not** track your location in the background or share it with anyone.
- We do **not** transmit your data to our servers or any third party.
- The app has **no** third-party SDKs that receive your data.

## 3. How your information is used

Your data is used **only** to provide the app's features on your device,
including:

- Starting, ending, and editing work sessions and breaks.
- Calculating earnings from your hourly rate and currency.
- Displaying dashboards and generating CSV, JSON, and PDF reports.
- Sending local reminders for long sessions, end-of-day checkout, and backups.
- Backing up and restoring your data when you choose to.

## 4. Permissions

- **Location** — requested only when you perform a tracking action, and only
  if location capture is enabled. Location capture is best-effort: denying
  permission, disabling location services, or a timeout never prevents you
  from tracking your time.
- **Notifications** — used only to show the local reminders described above.

You can revoke either permission at any time in your device settings.

## 5. Data storage and retention

Your data is stored only in the app's private on-device storage. It remains
there until you delete it. You can remove individual entries, clear stored
locations, or perform a full data reset from Settings at any time. Uninstalling
the app deletes all locally stored data.

## 6. Backups and data sharing

Backups and exports are initiated **only by you**. When you export a backup
(JSON) or a report (CSV, JSON, or PDF), the file is handled by your device's
share or file picker, and you choose where it is saved or shared. We do not
receive, access, or transmit these files.

## 7. Data sharing with third parties

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The
only time data leaves your device is when you explicitly export or share it
yourself.

## 8. Children's privacy

Simple Time Tracker is a general productivity tool and is not directed to
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. Because the app collects no personal information over a network, no
personal data of any user is transmitted to us.

## 9. Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device, you have full control over
it at all times. You can view, edit, export, or delete any of your data from
within the app. If you have questions or requests about your data, contact us
at hello@gl-labs.dev.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top
indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected here.

## 11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, contact us at
**hello@gl-labs.dev**.
